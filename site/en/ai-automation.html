FAQ

Answers up front

Do I need my own IT team or coding skills?

No. We handle development, setup and operation. You only need access to the systems we connect.

What happens to our data?

In the intro call we clarify which data is processed and where. The solution is built to match your data protection requirements, including a data processing agreement.

Does the AI make decisions on its own?

Only if you want it to. Usually the AI prepares and a person approves, for example for quotes or replies to customers.

How much does an automation cost?

After the intro call you get a fixed price. Smaller automations are often done within a few days.
