FAQ

Answers up front

Which systems can data come from?

Excel and CSV, common ERP and CRM systems, databases and many cloud services with an API.

Who can see the dashboard?

Only authorised people. Access is protected by login, with different views per role on request.

How often is the data updated?

Depending on the source: hourly, daily or close to real time.

Do we need an expensive BI tool?

No. We build the dashboard as a web application, with no ongoing licence fees per user.
