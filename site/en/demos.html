GeoAuftrag on the phone

How field crews report from the site

The crew view runs in the browser on any phone, no app store needed. Try it right here: you are signed in as technician Ahmet Demir.

  1. 1

    Open the job

    “Today” lists the day’s jobs with address, due date and a route to Google Maps.

  2. 2

    Report on site

    One tap on “Vor Ort melden” measures the location by GPS and checks it is within the site’s tolerance radius.

  3. 3

    Photos and hours

    Photos get time, coordinates and job number stamped on them. Add working hours and who was on site.

  4. 4

    Draw the cable route

    Walk or place cable routes and markers right on the site plan. The office sees everything at once and can invoice.

  5. 5

    Book materials

    Book materials used, such as 10 t of sand or 100 m of copper cable, right with the report. The client sees planned versus actual per site and confirms the quantities.
