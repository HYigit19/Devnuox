FAQ

Answers up front

How long does a new website take?

A business website with a few pages is usually ready in two to three weeks. You see the first draft after about a week.

Can I edit content myself later?

Yes. On request we set up simple editing, or we handle changes as part of our support.

Where is the website hosted?

On a fast, global network. Hosting and domain can be in your account or ours, whichever you prefer.

Do I need my own domain?

For launch, yes. Until then you see the website on a free preview address.
